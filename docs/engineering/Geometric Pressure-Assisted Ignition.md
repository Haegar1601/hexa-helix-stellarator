## The Core Idea: Pressure as Density Booster

The Lawson criterion requires:  
**n × T × τ_E > threshold**  

(where n = plasma density, T = temperature, τ_E = energy confinement time).

Conventional approaches maximize T (extreme heating) to compensate for moderate n.  

**HexaHelix inversion:** Sustained multi-GPa pressure (from B-N-C strain + 6-helix geometry) increases n dramatically—allowing T to be reduced while maintaining the product.
